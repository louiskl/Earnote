Your lectures and meetings, written up.

Earnote records, transcribes with Whisper and turns the result into structured notes — all on your Mac. No account, no subscription, nothing in the cloud.

What it does

Records what matters

Microphone and system audio together, so the other people in Zoom, Teams or Meet are on the recording too. No virtual audio device to install.

Notes you can actually use

A short summary, the topics that came up, decisions, and tasks as checkboxes — not a wall of transcript.

Listen back to any sentence

Every timestamp in the transcript is a button: click it and you hear that exact moment. Useful when a term was hard to catch.

Fix a name once

Correct a misheard name or term and Earnote fixes it in the title, note and transcript — and remembers it for the next recording.

Made for two kinds of days

Studying

Record a 90-minute lecture, get a study sheet you can print as a PDF. Terms from your dictionary are spelled correctly, the exam date ends up in the task list, and you can jump back into the audio wherever the lecturer went fast.

Meetings and calls

Earnote notices when a call starts and offers to take notes. Afterwards you get decisions and who agreed to do what — ready to file in Notion, Obsidian, Apple Notes, Bear, Craft or a plain Markdown folder.

It stays on your Mac

Transcription runs locally with Whisper. The notes are written by a language model that Earnote downloads once and then runs on your Mac — no account, no API key, and it works offline. Audio, transcripts and notes live in your home folder and are never synced anywhere.

If you prefer a cloud AI (Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, Mistral, your own server), you can choose one in the settings. Earnote tells you clearly when the transcript would leave your Mac. What the app does with your data (German).

Before you start

Earnote needs macOS 15 or newer on Apple Silicon (M1 and later) and about 8 GB of memory. On the first run it downloads the speech model (~1.6 GB) and the local AI (~2.3 GB) and prepares them for your chip — that happens once and takes a few minutes.

Recording other people usually requires their consent (in Germany, § 201 StGB). Earnote shows a reminder before each recording; the responsibility is yours.

Questions

Is it really free?
Yes. Free, open source under the MIT licence, no accounts, no paid tier. It is a student project, not a business.
How fast is it?
On an M1 MacBook Air, a 60-minute lecture is transcribed in about five minutes — and because Earnote already transcribes while you record, the note is usually ready a few minutes after you stop.
Does it work with Zoom, Teams and Meet?
Yes. Earnote captures the system audio alongside your microphone, so everyone on the call ends up in the transcript.
Which languages?
The interface follows your Mac: English or German. Recordings can be transcribed in many languages, and you choose the language of the notes separately.
Can I edit the notes?
Yes — edit the text, summarise again with different instructions, or go back to the AI version with one click.
Why isn’t it in the App Store?
The sandbox rules there would get in the way of recording system audio and filing notes into other apps. The download here is signed and notarised by Apple, so it opens with a double-click.
